## 15 Multi-Year Recipe Staples (25–30 Year Shelf Life)
*Note: Store dry goods in 5-gallon food-grade buckets with 7-mil Mylar bags and oxygen absorbers.*

### 1. Hard White Wheat Berries
- **Shelf Life:** 25–30 years
- **Recipe Versatility:** Grind into fresh whole wheat flour for daily breads, tortillas, pancakes, and pizza dough, or boil whole like rice/barley for a hearty grain base.

### 2. Long-Grain White Rice
- **Shelf Life:** 25–30 years (oxygen-free)
- **Recipe Versatility:** Neutral starch base for stir-fries, rice bowls, pilafs, soups, congee (great for toddlers), and casseroles.

### 3. Pinto & Black Beans
- **Shelf Life:** 25–30 years
- **Recipe Versatility:** Essential plant protein for refried beans, chili, Mexican-style bowls, bean soups, and burger/croquette patties.

### 4. Rolled Oats
- **Shelf Life:** 25–30 years
- **Recipe Versatility:** Morning oatmeal, baking cookies, thickening stews, making oat milk, savory oat porridge, and meatloaf binders.

### 5. Dried Pasta (Penne, Macaroni, Spaghetti)
- **Shelf Life:** 10–25+ years
- **Recipe Versatility:** Base for bakes, noodle soups, cheesy stovetop pasta, cold pasta salads, and quick skillet meals.

### 6. Granulated White Sugar
- **Shelf Life:** Indefinite (keep moisture-free)
- **Recipe Versatility:** Baking, sweetening oatmeal, yeast activation for breads, preserving homemade jams, and balancing savory sauces.

### 7. Salt (Iodized & Kosher/Sea Salt)
- **Shelf Life:** Indefinite
- **Recipe Versatility:** Flavor enhancement, fermentation, food curing, and basic cooking chemistry.

### 8. Dehydrated / Powdered Whole Milk
- **Shelf Life:** 15–20 years
- **Recipe Versatility:** Reconstitutes for drinking or cereal, baking, creamy sauces, gravies, mac-and-cheese, and toddler porridges.

### 9. Whole Egg Powder
- **Shelf Life:** 5–10+ years
- **Recipe Versatility:** Scrambled eggs, baking cakes and breads, making pancakes, binding meatloaves, and coating cutlets.

### 10. Dehydrated Potato Flakes & Slices
- **Shelf Life:** 10–25 years
- **Recipe Versatility:** Quick mashed potatoes, potato soups, gnocchi dough, and a natural thickener for stews and gravies.

### 11. Dehydrated Mixed Vegetables (Carrots, Peas, Onions, Peppers)
- **Shelf Life:** 15–25 years
- **Recipe Versatility:** Rehydrates into soups, stews, fried rice, omelets, and savory pot pies.

### 12. Freeze-Dried Meats (Chicken, Beef, Tuna)
- **Shelf Life:** 15–25 years
- **Recipe Versatility:** Adds high-quality protein to pastas, rice bowls, casseroles, sandwiches, and pet food toppers.

### 13. Bouillon Granules (Chicken, Beef, Vegetable)
- **Shelf Life:** 5–10 years
- **Recipe Versatility:** Rich broths, gravies, seasoning rice/beans, and soup bases.

### 14. Bulk Cooking Fats (Avocado Oil / Powdered Shortening)
- **Shelf Life:** 2–5 years (oils, rotate regularly) to 10+ years (powdered shortening)
- **Recipe Versatility:** Sautéing, frying, roasting, and adding critical calorie density to meals.

### 15. Honey
- **Shelf Life:** Indefinite (naturally unspoilable)
- **Recipe Versatility:** Natural sweetener, bread spread, meat glaze, and soothing throat additive.
