## DIY Activated Charcoal for Off-Grid Water Filtration

*Important Safety Warning: DIY charcoal filters remove chemical toxins, pesticides, heavy metals, and bad tastes/odors via adsorption, but do **NOT** reliably kill microscopic viruses or bacteria. Always boil or UV/chemically purify filtered water before drinking.*

### Step 1: Charcoal Pyrolysis (Pure Hardwood Charcoal)
*Do NOT use commercial charcoal briquettes—they contain toxic lighter fluid and chemical binders.*
1. **Materials:** Clean, untreated hardwood (oak, maple, hickory) or coconut shells cut into 1–2 inch chunks.
2. **Setup:** Pack wood tightly inside a non-coated metal retort (e.g., clean paint can or stainless steel drum). Drill 3–4 small gas vent holes in the lid.
3. **Burn:** Place container in an outdoor fire for 3–5 hours until smoke/gas stops escaping from the vent holes.
4. **Cool:** Let cool completely overnight. Inspect to ensure wood is solid black and brittle throughout.

### Step 2: Crushing & Washing
1. **Grind:** Crush charcoal into coarse granules (roughly coarse coffee ground size). Avoid fine dust (clogs filter) or large chunks (low surface area).
2. **Rinse:** Thoroughly rinse crushed charcoal with clean water to remove loose ash.

### Step 3: Chemical Activation
1. **Solution:** Prepare a 25% solution of Calcium Chloride (e.g., Ball Pickle Crisp) mixed into clean water (100g Calcium Chloride per 300mL water). *Alternative: Pure lemon juice or non-iodized sea salt solution in an emergency.*
2. **Soak:** Submerge crushed charcoal in the solution and soak for 24 hours.
3. **Drain:** Drain liquid through cheesecloth/mesh sieve and rinse with purified water.

### Step 4: Baking & Storage
1. **Bake:** Spread damp charcoal on a baking sheet and bake at **225°F–250°F (110°C–120°C) for 2 to 3 hours** to drive off moisture and open pores.
2. **Store:** Immediately store in airtight glass jars with desiccant packs to prevent it from absorbing ambient moisture/odors.

### 4-Tier Emergency Gravity Filter Stack
1. **Top Layer:** Coarse Gravel *(removes leaves, twigs, large debris)*
2. **Second Layer:** Fine Sand *(traps floating particles & sediment)*
3. **Third Layer:** **DIY Activated Charcoal Granules** *(adsorbs chemicals, toxins, odors, bad taste)*
4. **Bottom Layer:** Cotton Cloth / Coffee Filter *(prevents charcoal dust in output water)*
